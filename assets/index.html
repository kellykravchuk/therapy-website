Approach

Thoughtful therapy that goes beneath the surface without losing practicality.

Kelly’s work integrates psychodynamic and relational therapy with attachment-based, trauma-informed care. The focus is not just on symptom relief, but on understanding patterns, strengthening regulation, and creating more room for connection, choice, and self-trust.

Depth with steadiness

Sessions make room for the deeper story while staying grounded in what helps you feel more resourced now.

Relational and attachment-focused

Therapy explores how early relationships and present environments shape self-protection, intimacy, and identity.

Developmentally informed

Work with children, teens, and families reflects each person’s stage of development, nervous system, and communication style.

Holistic by design

Sleep, movement, nourishment, social support, and environment are treated as real parts of emotional health, not afterthoughts.

Specialties

Support for the places people often look “fine” from the outside.

This practice is especially aligned with thoughtful, sensitive, self-aware people who want more than symptom management alone, as well as families needing attuned support around neurodivergence and regulation.

High-functioning anxiety

For people who appear capable and composed while privately carrying overthinking, perfectionism, pressure, or chronic self-criticism.

Dating and relationships

Space to work with attachment patterns, mixed signals, emotional unavailability, boundaries, and the strain of modern dating.

Neurodivergent families

Support for autism, ADHD, regulation differences, diagnosis questions, advocacy, parent coaching, and family stress.

Burnout and perfectionism

Therapy for the tension between ambition and depletion, especially when productivity has become tied to worth.

Children, teens, and young adults

Developmentally responsive care for overwhelm, emotional pain, identity questions, transitions, and family conflict.

Parent support

Coaching that strengthens co-regulation, attuned communication, boundaries, and the emotional climate of the home.

Therapy can be a place to slow down enough to hear yourself again.

Practice Details

Clear information for taking the next step.

The goal is a simple, low-pressure path into care. If you are unsure whether the practice is a fit, a short consultation can help clarify that.

Where are sessions available?

In-person sessions are offered in Hermosa Beach. Telehealth is available across California.

Who is the practice for?

Children, teens, adults, couples, and parents seeking warm, thoughtful, specialized support.

Do you offer a consultation?

Yes. A free 15-minute consultation is available to discuss what is bringing you in and whether the practice feels like a good fit.

What about fees and insurance?

The practice is out-of-pocket. Superbills are available for possible out-of-network reimbursement, and limited sliding-scale spots may be available.
