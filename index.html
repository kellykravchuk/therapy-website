My Approach

I approach therapy with curiosity, warmth, and care, working collaboratively at a pace that feels safe enough for honest exploration. My work integrates psychodynamic and relational therapy with attachment-based and trauma-informed care.

I specialize in developmental work with neurodivergent youth and families—including parent coaching and DIR/Floortime-informed support—as well as therapy for adolescents and adults navigating anxiety, perfectionism, burnout, dating stress, and life transitions.

Clinically, I’m interested in how early relationships live on inside of us and shape the ways we protect ourselves: the roles we take on, the patterns we repeat, and the ways our environments and relationships influence our sense of self. I pair insight with practical support for regulation, boundaries, and more authentic connection.

I also take a holistic view of wellness, attending to sleep, movement, nourishment, social connection, and the conditions that help people feel more alive, steady, and like themselves.
